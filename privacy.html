Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes how Team Tool Hub handles information when you visit the Tool Hub website and use its file tools.

Files, OCR, and saved drafts

Tool Hub's file tools process selected files in your browser. The Malayalam OCR tool also recognizes images in your browser; images and recognized text are not sent to an AI service. Your browser downloads the OCR engine and Malayalam language data from a third-party CDN when you start OCR.

Text files and DOCX files selected for import are read in your browser. Drafts you choose to save are stored in that browser's local storage and remain there until you delete them or clear the browser's site data. They are not synced to a Tool Hub account.

Google Analytics

Tool Hub uses Google Analytics to understand site usage. The Analytics tag is loaded only if you allow Google Analytics in the site's privacy choices. Google may process information such as browser and usage data under its own terms and privacy policy. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Malayalam text and OCR

AI proofreading is currently unavailable. Text entered or recognized by the Malayalam tool stays in your browser unless you choose to save a draft, which stores it in that browser's local storage. OCR images are processed in your browser and are not sent to an AI service. Your browser downloads the OCR engine and Malayalam language data from a third-party CDN when you start OCR.

Advertising

Tool Hub uses Google AdSense to display advertising. The AdSense tag and ad placement are loaded only if you allow advertising in the site's privacy choices. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, and measure ads, subject to your choices and applicable requirements. Learn more in Google's advertising policies.

Cookies and privacy choices

The site stores your optional Google service choices in your browser's local storage so it can remember them. You can change your choice from the Cookie settings link. Clearing your browser's site data also removes the saved choice. Google services may use cookies or similar technologies when enabled.

Third-party resources

The site loads fonts and software libraries from third-party providers. A request to those providers may disclose standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver the requested resource. Those providers handle that information under their own privacy policies.

Children

Tool Hub is a general-purpose utility site and is not directed at children.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email besttoolkit@gmail.com.
